SpeedPy Base64 Tool

A simple tool to encode and decode Base64 strings

This is a fresh SpeedPy fork with a simplified interface. The main page now serves as a Base64 encoder/decoder tool.

You can access other pages through the navigation menu or by visiting:

  • Dashboard

How to Use

Simply enter text in the input field and click either "Encode to Base64" or "Decode from Base64" to convert your text.

Features

  • Real-time Base64 encoding and decoding
  • Supports UTF-8 characters
  • Responsive design for all devices
  • Clear functionality to reset the form
